Sources

Everything is processed in your browser. No line is sent anywhere.

Drop your logs here

Drag in one or more files, paste text with Ctrl+V, or use “Add logs”. Each file becomes a source; Tresse recognizes the timestamps and interleaves the lines.

      Color rules

      Shift+Enter or Color turns the query into a color rule: instead of hiding the other lines, it colors the ones it matches. A line matching several rules shows one bar per rule on its left, and takes the background of the first one. Click a rule to turn it off, its count to jump to the next match, ✎ to edit it.
